## RED FLAGS & TROUBLESHOOTING

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| No improvement from any method | Data leakage or wrong seed | Verify grouped-split is deterministic, check seed |
| Healthy FP increases | Copy-paste or aggressive augmentation | Reduce copy_paste value, use conservative HSV |
| Results vary by seed | Small data variance | Phase F (multi-seed) is essential |
| CLAHE causes artifacts | Parameters too aggressive | Reduce clip_limit to 2.0, tile_size to 8 |
| Macenko normalization too slow | Preprocessing bottle neck | Switch to Reinhard (faster alternative) |
| RandAugment shows no gain | Policy not suitable | Try manual tuning or phase in later |
