# Hardware provenance policy

The collected runtime files describe the hardware that actually produced or
audited the current artifacts.

A future Kaggle T4×2 configuration may be supplied as a **reproduction target**,
but results generated on an RTX 4090 must not be represented as having been
generated on Kaggle T4×2.

For a paper table labelled "Kaggle T4×2", rerun the corresponding final
training/evaluation workflow on Kaggle T4×2 and store its runtime evidence.
