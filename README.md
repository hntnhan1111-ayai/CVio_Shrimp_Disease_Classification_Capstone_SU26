# CVio_Shrimp_Disease_Classification_Capstone_SU26

Kho luu tru notebook nay duoc to chuc theo 3 nhom chinh:

- `best`: chua notebook tot nhat tinh den hien tai, tuc pipeline dat hieu suat cao nhat va duoc xem la phien ban on dinh de tham khao hoac trien khai tiep.
- `experiment`: chua cac notebook dung trong qua trinh thu nghiem, tinh chinh va danh gia cac pipeline khac nhau truoc khi chon ra phien ban tot nhat.
- `legacy`: chua cac notebook cu, cac phien ban truoc day hoac notebook khong con duoc uu tien su dung nhung van duoc giu lai de doi chieu.

Cau truc nay giup tach ro notebook dang su dung, notebook dang nghien cuu va notebook da loi thoi, tu do de quan ly qua trinh phat trien mo hinh de hon.
