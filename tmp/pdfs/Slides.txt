1. Project introduction
Problem Statement
Disease outbreaks can cause substantial
production loss and instability.
Laboratory inspection may be unavailable, delayed, or costly in resource-
constrained settings.
Visual disease detection dependent on farmer experience
may be inaccurate and unstable 
Disease symptoms can be small, sparse, visually overlapping, affected by
difficult environmental conditions \n\n7
1. Project introduction
Research Gap
Existing work
Shrimp disease studies primarily use image classification or bounding-box
detection.
Lightweight CNN and YOLO models have been explored.
Attention, robustness, and XAI have been investigated separately in related vision
tasks.
Gaps addressed
Limited disease-region masks for BG and WSSV.
Repeated-specimen leakage rarely quantified.
Healthy negative-image segmentation failures often hidden.
Limited combined evaluation of classification, segmentation, robustness, Fourier
processing, and mobile deployment.\n\n8
1. Project introduction
Objectives
develop deep-learning model for shrimp disease detection and localization
improve resilience against difficult environmental conditions such as lighting, blur,
noise, etc
optimize performance for mobile and resource-constraint deployment and inference
Application-oriented objectives:
Research-oriented objectives:
enrich the dataset for shrimp disease segmentation
 resolve specimen-leakage 
implement and evaluate attention-based enhancement 
implement and evaluate Fourier transform-based enhancement \n\n9
1. Project introduction
Project significance
Android mobile app for farmers to quickly screen shrimp disease detection 
Featuring: Image capture/upload; user-friendly prediction display and visual
evidence, history tracking
Lightweight models that are optimized for resource-constrained devices
Practical Contributions:
Scientific Contributions:
Integration of classification + segmentation in shrimp disease detection
Advances methods with attention mechanisms and Fourier-domain transformations
Evidence-based model selection (TIMM vs YOLO) under efficiency constraints
Data enrichment for subsequent studies, evaluate and resolve specimen-leakage \n\nScope Limitation
Predict Healthy, BG, WSSV, WSSV_BG, ranked by Macro-F1 Limited dataset size and generalization
Localize BG & WSSV disease regions Single annotator masks/no expert review
Convert selected models to TFLite for faster inference XAI is qualitative
Integrate into Android app that supports: image
capture/upload, inference and prediction display,
inspection history
Prototype app not validated with farmers
Experiments for robustness, explainability, fairness,
external datasets, attention and Fourier-domain
enhancements
Not a replacement for lab diagnosis or veterinary
assessment

 Nhu - \n\nThe Fourier Transform converts an image from the spatial domain into the frequency domain.
The spatial domain describes pixels by their position and intensity.
The frequency domain describes how rapidly image intensity changes across space.

Image space High-pass enhancement Strengthen small spots, edges and texture
Image space High-frequency damping Reduce excessive fine detail or noise
Image space Band-pass filtering Focus on a selected frequency scale
Image space Low-frequency flattening Reduce illumination and broad background variation
Image space Homomorphic filtering Adjust illumination–reflectance components

Image space W1 high-pass reference Selected controlled transform for further experiments
