# Claims and Limitations

## Safe Claims (seed-42 scope required)
- On the fixed seed-42 Stage-1 split, YOLO26m-cls with ASL-LDAM and SimAM-DCFR improved over the native CE baseline in Macro-F1 (+0.019937), accuracy (+0.023095), and Cohen's Kappa (+0.031093).
- The top-5 noisy conditions were selected based on the weakest CE baseline mean Macro-F1 across severities 1-3.
- The artifact package offers clean-test, top-5 noise, XAI, model comparison, and efficiency evidence for paper review.

## Claims to Avoid
- statistically significant
- state-of-the-art without explicit comparative benchmark outside this package
- robust across random seeds
- globally best across all architectures
- mean ± std over multiple seeds
- mobile deployment validated without direct mobile benchmark evidence
