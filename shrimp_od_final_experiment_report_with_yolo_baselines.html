3. What the baseline benchmark says

YOLO26n is best by strict box mAP50-95 in the uploaded benchmark: 0.0389. However, it has very poor image-level diagnosis performance at conf 0.25: Macro-F1 0.0987 and no-detection miss rate 89.2%.

YOLO11s is the most defensible practical baseline: it has the highest mAP50 in the 14-model benchmark, the best disease-level Macro-F1, and later becomes the strongest model under high-resolution re-evaluation.

YOLOv10n is the fastest model at 260.1 FPS, but it does not beat YOLO11s on detection quality.

Do not select the model only by mAP50-95. For this dataset, the box-level metric and image-level disease usability can disagree sharply.

6. Key interpretation

Real improvement from original locked baseline to fair 1280 baseline:
mAP50: +0.026517
mAP50-95: +0.005666

This is the only meaningful improvement so far. It comes from fair high-resolution inference and post-processing calibration, not from a new architecture.

The later best balanced setting only adds +0.000459 mAP50 and +0.000120 mAP50-95 over the fair anchor. That is too small to claim a method-level breakthrough.

7. Safe paper wording

A fair high-resolution re-evaluation shows that the YOLO11s baseline is strongly sensitive to inference resolution and post-processing parameters. Re-evaluating the same trained YOLO11s baseline at imgsz=1280 improves test mAP50 from 0.1308 to 0.1573 and mAP50-95 from 0.0378 to 0.0435. Additional confidence/max-det calibration yields only marginal gains, indicating that further progress likely requires label refinement, tile-aware supervision, or tiny-box localization improvements rather than inference-only tuning.

8. Final recommendation

The 14-model benchmark justifies selecting YOLO11s as the main baseline for the next milestone, not because it has the highest mAP50-95 in the raw benchmark, but because it has the strongest practical balance: best mAP50, best image-level diagnosis Macro-F1, and best performance after fair high-resolution re-evaluation.

Next real experiment: build labels_v2 from the audit queue, then retrain YOLO11s and evaluate using the calibrated setting imgsz=1280, conf=0.0005, IoU=0.50, max_det=600. The new model should beat 0.157789 mAP50 and 0.043617 mAP50-95 to count as a real improvement.
