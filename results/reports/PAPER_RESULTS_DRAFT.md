# Paper-ready results draft

YOLO11s-RECSRA achieved **16.036% mAP50** and **4.657% mAP50-95** on the matched clean test set, compared with **12.900%** and **3.800%** for the original YOLO11s benchmark baseline. This corresponds to **+3.136 pp** and **+0.857 pp**, or **+24.31%** and **+22.54%** relative improvements.

Across five severity levels, RECSRA retained positive mAP50 and mAP50-95 advantages for JPEG recompression, white-balance shift, low-light sensor noise, localized flash glare, and lens smudge. JPEG recompression showed the largest mean absolute mAP50-95 gain (**+0.532 pp**), while low-light sensor noise showed the largest relative gain (**+52.86%**).

Claims are limited to the stated metrics and protocol; they do not imply uniform superiority in mAP75, normalized retention, or external-domain performance.
