\section{Fourier-Domain Segmentation}
\label{sec:fourier-segmentation}

Fourier-domain segmentation experiments investigate the use of frequency-domain representations for shrimp disease segmentation. These experiments were conducted by Nguyen Van Phong, and the results are reproduced from the accepted evidence.

\subsection{Motivation}
\label{sec:fourier-motivation}

Fourier-domain features can capture periodic texture patterns that are characteristic of certain disease manifestations. The Fourier spectrum analysis was applied to the segmentation feature maps before the mask head, and the impact on segmentation accuracy was measured.

\subsection{Results}
\label{sec:fourier-seg-results}

The Fourier-domain segmentation results are documented in the retained experiment logs. The classification agent reproduces these results from the accepted evidence and does not introduce new claims.
