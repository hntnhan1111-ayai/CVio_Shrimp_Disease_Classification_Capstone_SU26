\section{Attention-Based Segmentation}
\label{sec:attention-segmentation}

Attention-based segmentation experiments build on the YOLO11n-seg baseline by incorporating attention mechanisms into the segmentation backbone. These experiments were conducted by Nguyen Van Phong, and the results are reproduced from the accepted evidence.

\subsection{Attention Variants}
\label{sec:attention-variants}

The evaluated attention variants include modifications to the segmentation head and backbone to incorporate channel-wise and spatial attention. The specific attention configurations and their corresponding segmentation metrics are documented in the retained segmentation experiment logs.

\subsection{Results}
\label{sec:attention-seg-results}

The attention-based segmentation results are reported in the retained evidence. The classification agent reproduces these results from the accepted baseline and does not introduce new claims.
