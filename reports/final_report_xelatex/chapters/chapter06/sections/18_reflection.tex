\section{Personal Reflection}
\label{sec:reflection}

This project has been a significant learning experience in applying deep learning to a real-world agricultural challenge. The work involved extensive experimentation with classification architectures, loss functions, and attention mechanisms, as well as collaboration on the segmentation component.

The primary challenge was balancing model capacity with the lightweight target for mobile deployment. The YOLO26m-cls backbone at 10.35M parameters proved to be an effective choice, and the ASL-LDAM + SimAM-DCFR components contributed measurable improvements. However, the negative integrated result and the preliminary nature of the mobile evidence serve as humbling reminders that field deployment remains a distant goal.

The project also highlighted the importance of disciplined experimental practice: machine-readable result logging, checkpoint hashing, and clear provenance tracking were essential for producing a reproducible evaluation. Future work should build on this foundation with broader validation, multi-seed evaluation, and real-world deployment testing.
