## KEY RESEARCH INSIGHTS

### Insight 1: Medical-Grade Color Normalization is Your Secret Weapon
Recent MICCAI 2024 papers (Gamper et al., Park et al.) show color normalization is critical for biological imaging. Aquaculture imaging varies by:
- Water quality (turbidity, algae)
- Lighting angles
- Tank conditions
- Camera settings

**Action**: Test both Macenko and Reinhard in Week 1. Expected +0.02-0.05 mAP50.

### Insight 2: Segmentation ≠ Classification Augmentation
Standard Mixup/CutMix destroy segmentation masks. ICCV 2024 papers show:
- **MaskMix** preserves boundaries while mixing
- **GridMask** acts as regularization
- **CopyPaste variants** improve boundary quality

**Action**: Skip aggressive mix augmentation. Focus on segmentation-safe variants.

### Insight 3: Small-Data Requires Careful Tuning
Your dataset (1149 images) needs:
- Conservative augmentation (not aggressive)
- Scheduling (strong early, weak late)
- Regularization (GridMask)

**Action**: Use Strong→Weak schedule in Phase B. Avoid aggressive color jitter.

### Insight 4: YOLO v11 Native Features Are Competitive
Ultralytics' RandAugment and segmentation-aware copy-paste are recent (2024) and proven:
- One-line config changes
- Native mask handling
- Tested by community

**Action**: Start with RandAugment (highest priority, lowest risk). Week 1, Day 1.

### Insight 5: Multi-Seed Validation Is Non-Negotiable
Nature Methods 2024 and CVPR 2024 papers show single-seed results unreliable for small data.

**Action**: Phase F (multi-seed confirmation) is not optional. Plan 30-50 GPU hours.
