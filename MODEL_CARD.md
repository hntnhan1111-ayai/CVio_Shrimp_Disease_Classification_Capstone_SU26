# Model card: YOLO11s-RECSRA

## Intended use

Research and prototype detection of BG and WSSV manifestations in onshore mobile-phone images of individual shrimp.

## Performance

- Clean mAP50: **16.036%**
- Clean mAP50-95: **4.657%**
- Precision: **24.342%**
- Recall: **24.695%**

## Out-of-scope

Autonomous veterinary diagnosis, underwater pond-wide detection, or deployment on unvalidated species, farms, devices, or diseases.

See `docs/LIMITATIONS.md`.
